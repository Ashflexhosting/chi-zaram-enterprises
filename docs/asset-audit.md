## Current usage guidance

The hero and pack visuals should remain product-led and high contrast. People-led imagery belongs on the Story page, while the transparent emblem is reserved for small identity moments over photography. New approved uploads can now be added through the owner Asset Library and assigned to the same categories before they are incorporated into public page content.
